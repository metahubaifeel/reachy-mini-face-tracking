## 一、英文主推，单条

Smooth face tracking has almost nothing to do with face detection.

## 二、英文线程版，想展开的时候用

**1/5**
Smooth face tracking has almost nothing to do with face detection.

Detection answers one question: where in the frame. That's it. Every jerky demo you've seen was sampling it too slowly, not detecting it badly.

**2/5**
Decision 1: close the full error every frame, not half.

"The face is off, correct 45% of it" hides your convergence rate inside the gain. Standing still you can't tell. The moment the person walks, you fall behind.

**3/5**
Decision 2: compute the target from where the head WAS when the frame was captured.

Capture to result takes ~55 ms and the head is still turning. Use the current angle instead and every frame overshoots a little. That's the oscillation you see.

**4/5**
Decision 3 and 4: adaptive smoothing, and the body never auto-recenters.

Track hard when the face moves, filter hard when it's still. And solve it with the wrist before reaching for the waist — a recentering body lurches the whole view.

**5/5**
Measured, same person, same motion, p90 following error:

naive 36.6°
this 9.6°

400 lines of dependency-free Python. Demo runs on a Reachy Mini, but the code binds to no robot SDK.

github.com/metahubaifeel/smoothgaze


## 三、中文单条

机器人头部跟脸跟得丝滑，跟人脸识别几乎没有关系。

真正让它顺的是另外四件事。实测横穿画面时跟随误差 p90：36.6 度 → 9.6 度。
